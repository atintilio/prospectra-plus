# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (no build step), hosted on Hostinger Web Hosting (hPanel, Apache/LiteSpeed + PHP). Contact form posts to `enviar.php` (PHP `mail()`). The 3D eye uses three.js r128 from cdnjs. Clean URLs and HTTPS via `.htaccess`.

## Users

Medium and large Brazilian companies taxed under Lucro Real or Lucro Presumido. Not only the CFO: everyone involved in costs, performance, compliance and savings — finance and controllership, tax, legal, accounting, partners and boards. They arrive evaluating whether Argus Prime can read their operation and point to risk, recoverable credit or structure to optimize.

## Product Purpose

Argus Prime is a financial and tax engineering hub. Purpose (brand manual): transform financial and tax complexity into safe, efficient and defensible decisions for companies operating in high-demand environments. Success for the site: a qualified visitor requests the diagnosis ("Mapear Oportunidades da Operação" / "Solicitar diagnóstico").

## Positioning

A hub that integrates finance, taxes and strategy into a single business reading (financeira, tributária, societária read together, no blind spots). Promise: "Decisões relevantes deixam de depender de estimativas."

## Operating Context

Work happens alongside the client's finance director, legal and accounting, close to the operation. Process: Mapear → Estruturar → Documentar → Acompanhar. Diagnosis is the entry point; a partner reads each contact message and replies by e-mail.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three fronts: Engenharia financeira (estrutura de capital, dívidas, valuation, risco, modelagem), Engenharia tributária (planejamento, recuperação e compensação de créditos, compliance; cessão onerosa de créditos judiciais homologados), Estratégia societária (fusões, aquisições, reorganização).
- Never promises results or absence of risk; every alternative ships with premises and risks declared.
- LGPD consent required on the contact form. Information treated with confidentiality.

## Brand Commitments

Manual de Marca v1.0 (Oct 2026) is binding: DM Serif Display (titles, highlight phrases, monumental numerals; never long text) + Manrope (body, 400–700). Colors: Charcoal #2D2923, Off-white #FBFAF7, Sand #F3EEE5, Dourado #C29A5D (only for rules, numerals and one emphasis per page), Marrom texto #4A423A, Marrom profundo #241C14. Gold 2px rule marks the start of each page. Sand panels without rounded corners. Official "olho digital" always on a dark background. Brown-to-off-white gradient on theme-opening pages. Logo: gold hexagonal AP symbol + ARGUS PRIME signature, never boxed, no shadows/glows/3D on the logo. Photography real, warm, partially desaturated, light vignette; avoid cold blue stock. Voice: like an experienced partner; short sentences; concrete verbs (conectar, mapear, estruturar, documentar, acompanhar); numbers with stated basis; zero self-promotion. Avoid: revolucionário, disruptivo, resultado garantido, o melhor do mercado, solução mágica.

User direction (binding for this redesign): keep the visual standard of the live site (off-white, serif headline with gold italic emphasis, charcoal button, eye card on dark), and make the eye come alive — real 3D depth and movement that leaps off the screen, cinematic scroll — across a full multi-page site.

## Product Principles

1. Base técnica explícita antes de qualquer recomendação.
2. Leitura integrada das três frentes, sem pontos cegos.
3. Discrição e confidencialidade.
4. Recomendação aplicável, não relatório de gaveta.
5. Nada de promessa que a operação não possa verificar.
